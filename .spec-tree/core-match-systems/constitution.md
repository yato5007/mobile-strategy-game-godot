## Scope Charter

This node turns the `Banner of the Majlis` concept into a planning-level core match rules frame. It must define the match flow, simultaneous planning/action model, legal action categories, Claim resolution approach, public information expectations, time/round structure under 30 minutes, resource/constraint direction, and required depth-2 branches.

## Non-Negotiable Principles

- The match is fixed-length; victory is evaluated at the final Majlis reveal.
- Claim must remain visible, explainable, and game-object-like, not a spreadsheet score.
- No player waits through full turns while others play; decisions happen in simultaneous planning windows and resolved action beats.
- Active play is rewarded through visible pressure and incentives; passive hiding must not dominate.
- Comebacks remain possible without arbitrary rubber-banding.
- The system must support 1v1v1v1 FFA and 2v2 team modes later.
- Match length must remain under 30 minutes.
- Arabic-first Majlis identity informs terminology and feedback, but this node does not create assets.
