# Constitution — Final Petition Pacing

## Non-Negotiables

- Phases 7-9 form the Final Petition act of the 9-phase Banner of the Majlis match.
- Victory is NOT awarded during any individual phase 7-9; only the Final Majlis Reveal after phase 9 determines the winner.
- No early match termination mechanism is permitted.
- All pacing must preserve strategic simultaneous planning, not reaction speed.
- FFA and 2v2 must both be supported without mode-specific timing forks.
- Portrait mobile at 360px logical width must be readable in Arabic and English.
- No Godot implementation, assets, or bots are created at this planning depth.

## Principles

1. **Urgency without panic**: Phases 7-9 accelerate tension while keeping decisions deliberate.
2. **Final counterplay window**: Players must have one clear moment to adjust before the last planning phase.
3. **Window-closing signal**: The approach of the Final Majlis Reveal must be visually undeniable.
4. **No table-only pressure**: Tension must be expressed through game objects, motion, and icon cues.
5. **Readable future path**: Players must see what is possible in the remaining phases, not what is locked.
