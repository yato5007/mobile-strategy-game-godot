# Clarification: FFA Final Standings Board

## Why show all four players?
FFA motivation depends on comparison. Second, third, and fourth place need to see how close they were and what separated them from higher ranks.

## Does fourth place get a negative label?
No. The board shows rank and claim count neutrally. Loss-learning details belong to the win/loss explanation subtree, not insulting rank copy.

## Are claim histories full timelines?
No. This board uses summaries. Full postmortem or detailed timeline may exist elsewhere. The board should stay compact.

## How are ties displayed?
A tie badge appears beside ranks, and a localized tie explanation line is reserved. Exact tie-break logic is owned by future core rules.

## Should the local player row move to the top?
No. Ranking order must remain true. The local player is highlighted in place so standings remain honest.

## What if player names are long?
Names wrap or truncate with tooltip-like future detail, but claim count and rank remain visible.

## Is this reusable for 2v2?
No. 2v2 has a separate team contribution display. Reusing the FFA board for 2v2 would weaken team identity.

## Localization Clarification
Rank labels, claim summary templates, and tie labels must be localized. Do not concatenate “Rank ” + number in player-facing copy.
