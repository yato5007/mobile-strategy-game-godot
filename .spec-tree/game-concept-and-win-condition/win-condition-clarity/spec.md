# Specification — Win Condition Clarity

## Purpose
Make “highest visible Claim at final Majlis wins” understandable before, during, and after the match.

## Player-Facing Win Statement
“Build the strongest Claim before the final Majlis by completing the public objectives shown on the board.”

## Required Clarity Moments
1. **Start of match:** show the final goal and current public objectives.
2. **During match:** show Claim leader, close contenders, and objective opportunities.
3. **Late match:** show what can still change the result.
4. **End:** show the winning Claim sources and decisive objective moments.

## Required Depth-3 Children
1. `claim-progress-display` — how progress and leader threat are represented.
2. `win-loss-explanation` — how the final result explains why players won/lost.
3. `team-and-ffa-win-readability` — how 2v2 and FFA readability differ.

Each depth-3 child must reach depth 4.
