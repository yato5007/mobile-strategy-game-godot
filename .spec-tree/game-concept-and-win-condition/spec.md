# Specification — Game Concept and Win Condition

## Product Question

What strategic promise and win condition should guide the game so that every later system serves a clear, fun, readable goal?

## Selected Concept Direction

The branch selects a **fixed-length strategic contest around visible public objectives and a final ceremonial victory reveal**, provisionally named:

```text
Banner of the Majlis
```

This is not a final title. It is a design direction:

- Players are rival leaders competing to prove their claim before the final Majlis.
- Each match has a fixed number of phases/rounds, so the game does not end early.
- Players gain visible claim through public, changing objectives rather than a hidden score race.
- The winner is the player/team with the strongest claim at the final reveal.
- Claim must be represented visually as banners, seals, influence tokens, route markers, or ceremonial progress—not as a plain table.

## Win Condition Statement

At match end, the player/team with the highest visible Claim wins. Claim is earned by completing public strategic objectives during the match. The match always reaches its final phase unless a future branch documents a true technical/design blocker.

## Why This Is Clear

- “Earn the strongest Claim before the final Majlis” is readable as a goal.
- Public objectives show what to do now.
- A visible Claim track/banner display shows who is ahead.
- Final scoring explains which objectives produced the win.

## What This Branch Does Not Finalize

- Exact board/map structure.
- Exact objective types.
- Exact scoring formula.
- Exact number of rounds.
- Exact units/resources.
- Exact art style beyond Arabic-first ceremonial/strategic direction.

These must be resolved by child nodes and sibling branches.

## Depth-2 Children Required

This branch must split into these meaningful depth-2 nodes:

1. `win-condition-clarity` — makes the win/loss explanation and progress display concrete.
2. `strategic-tension-and-comeback` — defines how the lead can change without randomness or unfair rubber-banding.
3. `anti-dominant-strategy-and-active-play` — defines conceptual safeguards against one perfect plan and passive hiding.
4. `concept-presentation-and-theme` — ensures the concept becomes a readable game space with Arabic-first feel, not text boxes.

Each depth-2 node must reach depth 4 with full Spec Kit.

## Acceptance Criteria

- The concept is strategic and readable.
- The win condition can be explained in one sentence.
- The match remains open until final phases.
- Comeback and anti-passive requirements are explicit.
- Presentation requirements block spreadsheet-like execution.
- Child nodes are meaningful and sufficient for depth-4 expansion.
